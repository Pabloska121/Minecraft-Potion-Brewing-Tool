How to Use This Minecraft Potion Tool

Step 1: Begin your potion brewing journey in Minecraft by selecting the base type: Awkward, Splash, or Lingering potion. This is the foundation for all brewing recipes.

Step 2: Use the potion dropdown menu to choose the specific potion you want to craft, such as Regeneration, Swiftness, or Fire Resistance. Each option unlocks a different recipe path.

Step 3: Apply modifiers to your potion if needed — extend its duration or increase its power. This step lets you fully customize your potion effects before crafting.

Step 4: Click the Brew button to generate a detailed potion recipe. The tool will display all crafting steps and ingredients needed to brew your selected Minecraft potion.
